Put words on your photos the Good Cup way, for an Instagram carousel and Story. Everything stays in your browser. First time? Read the 5-step guide.

Load the Good Cup fonts

Checking this browser for the fonts...

2

Add your photos

One photo per slide, in posting order. JPG, PNG or HEIC (HEIC opens in Safari). Drag the photo on the preview to move it.

Or drop photos here
Zoom
3

Write the words

Press Return for a new line. Select some words to make only those bold, italic, Iowan or a colour. Drag the words on the preview to move them; arrow keys nudge 1 px, Shift + arrow 10 px.

Whole slide

Carousel and Story keep their own word and photo positions: check both with the switch above the preview.

A project file keeps the photos, words and positions. Keep it beside the post on the NAS.

5

Save it to the NAS

  1. Unzip the download. You get one folder named after the post.
  2. On GoodNAS, upload that folder into:
01_Final_Outputs / Photos / 00 Post Maker - Inbox

On a phone: the zip lands in Files (iPhone) or Downloads (Android). Unzipping is easier on a computer, so upload from a laptop when you can.

Upload only into that folder. Never move, rename or delete anything else on the NAS.
