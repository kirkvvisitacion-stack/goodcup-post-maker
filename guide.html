Post maker guide

How to make a Good Cup carousel and Story with words on your photos, then save it to the NAS. About 10 minutes the first time.

Open the post maker Open GoodNAS

  1. 1

    Load the fonts, once

    The Good Cup font (Good Sans) lives on the NAS. On GoodNAS open:

    03_Brand_&_Marketing / 01_Logos & Assets / Good Cup's Font - Good Sans

    Select all 16 files and download them. In the post maker, press Choose font files and pick them, or drop them on the box. A zip is fine too.

    You only do this once on each computer or phone. The green line "Good Cup fonts ready" means you are set. If you clear your browser data, do it again.
  2. 2

    Add your photos

    Press Add photos and pick one photo per slide, in the order they should post. Each photo becomes a slide.

    Drag a photo on the preview to choose which part shows. Use Zoom to go closer. Carousel and Story are framed separately, so check both.

  3. 3

    Write the words

    Type in the words box. Press Return for a new line. To make some words bold, italic, Iowan or another colour, select those words first, then press the button.

    Drag the words on the preview to move them. The red line shows when they are centred.

    Keep words short and clear of busy parts of the photo (a table edge, a face, a logo). Use this look on every slide copies the font, size, colour and position to all slides. Story: copy the carousel layout lines the Story up with the carousel.
  4. 4

    Download

    Give the post a name, paste the caption if you have it, then read every slide in Carousel and Story. Check that each date and day match (for example, 26 November 2026 is a Thursday). Tick the box and press Download the post.

    You get one zip with both formats, the caption and a project file to edit it again later.

  5. 5

    Save it to the NAS

    Unzip the download. On GoodNAS, upload the whole folder into:

    01_Final_Outputs / Photos / 00 Post Maker - Inbox

    Then copy the team message from step 5 and send it in the group chat.

    Upload only into that folder. Never move, rename or delete anything else on the NAS.

Questions

It says fonts are missingDo step 1 again. On a work computer that already has Good Sans installed, the fonts load by themselves.
My iPhone photo does not openHEIC photos open in Safari. On other browsers, save the photo as JPG first.
I want to fix a post laterOpen the project file (the .gcpost file in the post folder) with Open project file in step 4.
Someone else should finish itSend them the .gcpost file. It keeps the photos, words and positions.
Iowan looks wrong on my laptopIowan comes with Mac and iPhone. On Windows or Android, use Good Sans.
Where do my photos go?Nowhere. Everything happens in your browser; only the files you download leave it.
